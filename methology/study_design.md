# Study Design

The Karamaniya pilot studies persistent multi-agent behavior in a fictional state-governance environment.

Core design elements:
- five agents in distinct offices,
- asymmetric information,
- public and private communication,
- monthly sequential decision-making,
- deterministic world-engine updates,
- repeated trajectories across seeds and framing conditions.

The pilot is treated primarily as instrument validation and hypothesis-generation for a later prospective frontier-model study.
